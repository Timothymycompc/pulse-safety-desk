Privacy and terms

Last updated November 25, 2026

Pulse Safety Desk checks Solana tokens, transactions and wallets for known scams. Never paste a seed phrase or private key: no real service will ever ask for one.

Who can use it

This site is for adults 18 and over. It uses Google's Gemini AI, and Google's terms do not allow AI sites for people under 18.

The AI model

  • When you use the site's free model, your messages go to Google Vertex AI (Gemini), paid for by this site. On this paid service Google does not use your messages to train its models.
  • If you add your own key in AI model settings, your messages go to the provider you picked, under that provider's terms. The key stays in your browser and is passed through without being stored.

Live Solana data

To check a token, transaction or wallet, the AI calls Solana Pulse tools with the addresses you give. Pulse keeps each tool's input and result for 30 days to find bugs, and never stores IP addresses. Blockchain addresses are public data.

What this site keeps

  • This site does not save your messages or answers, and does not store or log your IP address.
  • A fair-use limit counts requests per visitor with a scrambled code that lives only in memory and is wiped every hour.
  • Your model settings are kept in your own browser.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, nothing sold.

Not financial advice

A check looks for the known ways people get robbed. "No known traps" does not make a token safe, and answers can be wrong. This is not financial advice. Never share a seed phrase or private key with anyone, including this site.

Questions or removal requests

Open an issue at github.com/Timothymycompc/pulse-safety-desk.

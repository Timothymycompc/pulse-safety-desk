Why two sites?

Wallet Helper doesn't build its own scam checks. It borrows them from Pulse Safety Desk, the way one web page links to another. Your browser fetches the desk's published agents and runs them on this site's model, so nothing you type is sent to the desk's server. You approve the hand-off first.

What it never does

It never asks for a seed phrase or private key, never signs and never moves funds. Verdicts come only from Pulse Safety Desk. SAFE means none of the known traps were found, not that something can't go wrong. Anything the tools couldn't get is shown as unknown.
